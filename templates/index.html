The evening

Three movements,
then the corridor.

No assembly, no bell. The Gold Hall keeps a ceremony small enough that a single page can fill it.

  1. 01

    The reading

    Ten minutes. A paper, a model, or a page read in full. The room does not interrupt.

  2. 02

    The defense

    Questions from the faculty panel, then one from the seats. Bring the homework, not a script for the questions.

  3. 03

    The award

    Marks are held until Wednesday at half past seven, when the hall hears them together.
